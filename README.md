## ZoneTwelve

I work on things that need to **run**, not just compile.

Most of my work focuses on **AI models, training and inference pipelines**, and the tooling required to evaluate, debug, and operate them in real environments.  
When existing tools don’t fit, I build small, focused solutions to close the gap.

I don’t strictly separate research, engineering, and infrastructure — if something blocks the work, I dig into it and fix it.

---

### What I usually work on
- AI model training, inference, and evaluation
- Performance and reliability issues in AI systems
- Tooling and infrastructure to support experiments and deployments
- Debugging unclear, slow, or partially working systems
